`sh.rnnn` ("somnolent-hogwash") is an RNNoise model from
https://github.com/GregorR/rnnoise-models, trained for speech recorded with
"recording" noise (fans, rooms, cheap microphones). Its authors state that the
models are not creative work and not subject to copyright.

It is used by rec0's audio processing through ffmpeg's `arnndn` filter.
